# Smart_Inventory_Demand_Prediction

Project Overview

This project predicts product demand using a Linear Regression model based on pricing, discounts, marketing spend, seasonal effects, and environmental factors. The goal is to help businesses optimize inventory, reduce stockouts, and avoid overstocking.

The model learns patterns from historical sales data and predicts future demand for smarter inventory planning.

---
Objectives
1. Predict product demand accurately
2. Understand price vs demand relationship
3. Analyze festival and marketing impact
4. Improve inventory decision-making
5. Reduce overstock and stockout risk

---
Key Insights
1. Higher discounts increase demand
2. Marketing spend positively impacts sales
3. Festival periods significantly boost demand
4. High base price reduces demand
5. Temperature slightly affects buying behavior
